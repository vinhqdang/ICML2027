\paragraph{The test on molecular targets.}
For DW-4 and LJ-13 the noised score has no closed form, so we apply the test of \cref{prop:diag}(i). We draw $N=20\,000$ reference configurations $x_0$ per noise level, form $x_t=x_0+\sigma_t\varepsilon$ in the centred subspace, and compute each training target exactly as in training ($K=100$, the TSI/DSI combination, gradient clipping on LJ-13). We use two test functions, the centred position $h(x)=x$ and the clipped force $h(x)=-\nabla\mathcal{E}(x)$ at $x_t$, and report the larger of the two $|z|$-scores of $\widehat D_h$ (\cref{tab:diag}). All estimators share the same $(x_0,\varepsilon)$, so their scores are correlated, and the reference itself comes from MCMC.
The outcome is the same on both systems. In this draw AEM passes at every noise level ($|z|\le2.2$ over 24 tests), as \cref{thm:unbiased} requires, and iDEM is rejected at every $\sigma_t\ge0.3$ on DW-4 ($|z|$ up to $39$) and every $\sigma_t\ge0.03$ on LJ-13 ($|z|$ up to $115$). Four further replicates, which redraw the configurations, the noise and the proposals, confirm this: iDEM is rejected in all five replicates at $\sigma_t\ge0.3$ on DW-4 and $\sigma_t\ge0.1$ on LJ-13 (smallest $|z|$ $15.7$ and $5.8$), but only in some at $\sigma_t=0.03$ on LJ-13 (median $|z|=2.4$). Over all $120$ AEM tests the largest $|z|$ is $3.2$; none survives a Bonferroni correction (threshold $3.6$), and the two values above $3$ occur on DW-4 at $\sigma_t\le0.1$, where the two targets nearly coincide and iDEM gives a similar value (same replicate).
Ten times more proposals reduce but do not remove the rejection ($|z|=4$--$18$ on DW-4 with $K=1000$). The test is one-sided in what it shows: iDEM's rejections are informative, but AEM's passes are partly built in, because at high noise its anchor carries almost all the weight and $T_{\mathrm{AEM}}\approx u$ (\cref{prop:diag}). At the lowest noise levels the test has little power, because the denoising target $u$ has variance of order $\sigma_t^{-2}$; there both estimators pass. As a sanity check on GMM-10, where the exact score is available, the exact score and AEM pass at $\sigma_t\in\{3,10\}$ and iDEM is rejected at $\sigma_t=10$ ($|z|=26.6$; \texttt{results/dsi\_bias}).
