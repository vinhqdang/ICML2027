
\paragraph{In two dimensions there is nothing to fix.}
On GMM-40 all four methods are statistically indistinguishable ($\mathcal{W}_2$ $4.6$--$5.0$, $\mathcal{E}$-$\mathcal{W}_1$ $0.80$--$0.92$, all 40 modes covered). This matches the estimator study: in $d=2$, SNIS with $K=100$ is already nearly unbiased at the noise levels that matter. The within-mode variance ratio of about $1.8$ is the same for every method, so it reflects the sampler discretization ($\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}=7000$ with 500 steps) and not the regression target.
